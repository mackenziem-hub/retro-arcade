RETRO ARCADE ON AN ANDROID PHONE
================================

The phone uses Lemuroid, a free all-in-one game app: box art library,
auto-save, touch controls, and Bluetooth controllers. It works offline.

1. INSTALL LEMUROID
   Play Store: search "Lemuroid" (by Swordfish90), or get it from F-Droid.

2. COPY THE GAMES TO THE PHONE (about 4.7 GB)
   a. Plug the phone into the computer with its USB cable.
      On the phone, tap the USB notification and choose "File transfer".
   b. On the phone's storage, make a folder called  Games
   c. From this arcade's "games" folder, copy these folders into it:
        Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance, NES, SNES,
        Nintendo 64, Nintendo DS, PlayStation
      Tight on space? Skip PlayStation (2.9 GB) and copy just the games you want.

3. POINT LEMUROID AT THE GAMES
   Open Lemuroid, and when it asks for a folder, pick the  Games  folder.
   It finds every game and adds the box art by itself.

SAVING
   Lemuroid auto-saves when you leave a game and resumes where you stopped.
   Phone saves stay on the phone. They do not sync with the computer arcade.

OPTIONAL
   GameCube games: install "Dolphin Emulator" from the Play Store and copy the
     "GameCube & Wii" folder too (9.6 GB). Needs a recent, powerful phone.
   Pokemon online (PokeMMO): install Extras\PokeMMO-Client.apk
     (allow "install unknown apps" when asked).
