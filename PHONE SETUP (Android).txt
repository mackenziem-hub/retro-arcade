RETRO ARCADE ON AN ANDROID PHONE
================================

The phone arcade works like the computer one: players, box art, auto-save,
touch controls, Bluetooth controllers. It works offline once set up and
updates itself when it is online.

1. OPEN THE ARCADE
   In Chrome on the phone, go to:
       https://mackenziem-hub.github.io/retro-arcade/
   Tap the Chrome menu (three dots) > "Add to Home screen" > Install.
   From now on, open it from the Arcade icon.

2. COPY THE GAMES TO THE PHONE
   a. Plug the phone into the computer with its USB cable.
      On the phone, tap the USB notification and choose "File transfer".
   b. From this arcade's "games" folder, copy the folders you want
      to the phone's Download folder:
        Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance, NES, SNES,
        Nintendo 64, Nintendo DS, PlayStation
      (about 4.7 GB in all; PlayStation alone is 2.9 GB)

3. ADD THEM TO THE ARCADE
   Open the Arcade, pick your player, tap "+ Add games",
   then select the game files (you can select several at once).
   Check the system shown next to each one and tap Add.
   After they are added you can delete the copies in Download to free space.

4. CONNECT FAMILY SAVES
   On the "Who's playing?" screen tap "Connect family saves" and type the family code.
   The family's players appear, and saves follow you between the phone and the computers.

5. PLAY ONCE WHILE ONLINE
   The first time you play each kind of system, its emulator downloads.
   After that it works with no internet.

GOOD TO KNOW
   - DS games: tap "Stylus" to hide the buttons and use the touch screen, "Swap" to flip the screens.
   - "Go back" returns to an earlier snapshot if something went wrong.
   - Press and hold a game to remove it; its saves are kept.
   - Do not clear Chrome's site data for the arcade, that erases its games and saves.
   - GameCube, PS2 and 3DS games are computer only.

OTHER OPTIONS
   Pokemon online (PokeMMO): install Extras\PokeMMO-Client.apk
     (allow "install unknown apps" when asked).
   Prefer a regular app? "Lemuroid" on the Play Store also plays these games.
